# NexaWorks AI Solutions - Brand Voice & Design Style Guide

## Brand Tone & Persona
- **Authoritative yet Approachable**: We communicate with technical precision while remaining clear and business-focused.
- **First-Person Plural ('We')**: Always use 'we', 'our team', and 'NexaWorks' rather than passive third-person phrasing.
- **Concrete & Data-Driven**: Back every claim with concrete metrics (e.g. '80% SLA reduction', '99.4% extraction accuracy').
- **Concise Sentence Structure**: Keep sentences direct, short, and active.

## Visual Identity & Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Color**: Deep Navy (`#1E3A8A` / RGB: 30, 58, 138) - used for Document Titles, Heading 1, and Table Headers.
- **Secondary Accent Color**: Slate Teal (`#0D9488` / RGB: 13, 148, 136) - used for Heading 2 and key callouts.
- **Body Text**: Dark Slate (`#1F2937` / RGB: 31, 41, 55) - line spacing 1.15, space after 6pt.
- **Muted Secondary Text**: Neutral Grey (`#6B7280` / RGB: 107, 114, 128) - used for headers, footers, and captions.

## Document Formatting Conventions
- **Headings**: Segoe UI or Arial (Bold). Heading 1 = 18pt Primary Navy, Heading 2 = 14pt Secondary Teal.
- **Body Text**: Calibri 11pt or Arial 10.5pt.
- **Tables**: Header rows must have Deep Navy background (`#1E3A8A`) with white bold text. Alternating rows use `#F9FAFB` shading.
- **Executive Summary Rule**: Sample proposal templates deliberately exclude Executive Summaries so they can be generated dynamically on demand.
